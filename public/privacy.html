Privacy Policy

This coursework project stores inventory information only in your browser. We do not send your product data to a backend server or to third-party analytics services.

What we store

  • Product titles, categories, locations, quantities, and dates that you add in the app.
  • Category names and category descriptions that you create.
  • Your language preference and your cookie consent choice.

How we use it

We use this information only to provide the inventory features of the app, keep your chosen language, and remember whether you accepted or declined the cookie banner.

Sharing and retention

This demo does not upload your data to a backend, and it does not share data with third parties. Your information remains on the device and browser profile that you use until you delete it or clear your browser storage.

Your choices

You can decline the cookie banner, change the language at any time, delete inventory records inside the app, or clear your browser cookies and local storage to remove saved data from this device.
